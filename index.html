03Personal productivity

NoteFlow — Note-Taking Web App

This full-stack note-taking app lets users create, edit, and organize notes with titles, categories, tags, and content, plus options like pinning, favoriting, and archiving.

  • Secure signup and login flows with account management, per-user data separation, and JWT-based security through a Spring Boot backend.
  • Dashboard search, filtering, sorting, and pagination help users quickly find and manage notes in a growing library.
  • Deployed the Spring Boot backend on Railway, connected data through MongoDB Atlas, and hosted the frontend on Netlify.

04MERN news reader

Briefly — Daily News Reader

A dark editorial-style news reader that brings ten essential daily headlines from a live news provider into one focused feed.

  • Uses an Express and MongoDB backend to keep the news provider API key server-side; readers can browse headlines freely, then sign in with JWT authentication and httpOnly cookies to open full articles.
  • Helps readers pick up where they left off, with built-in headline search and light and dark themes.
  • Designed with a charcoal palette, muted gold accents, and Playfair Display headlines, and deployed across Netlify, GitHub Pages, and Render.
